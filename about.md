# about me

<img class="pfp" src="{{ '/assets/images/grad.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="me at graduation">

I'm an astronomy student at the University of Canterbury, interested in time-domain astronomy, fast transients and sky surveys. My thesis work is on improving detection methods in TESS and analysing transients for the TESSELLATE sky survey.

- **Institution:** University of Canterbury
- **Supervisors:** Dr. Ryan Ridden-Harper, Dr. Joscha Jahns-Schindler, Assoc. Prof. Michael Albrow
- **Focus:** GRBs, FRBs, time-domain astronomy, simulations

[email](mailto:clarinda.montilla@gmail.com) · [github](https://github.com/crixiii) · [orcid](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-4935-069X) · [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/clarinda-m-49bb50274/)

Outside of academia, I spend much of my time pursuing artistic interests and being involved in my local community.

During my Honours and Masters study at the University of Canterbury, I helped build a close-knit cohort of astronomy students, colloquially the "Astro Girlies". I organised routine social events and Zoom calls, so that everyone felt supported from working on assignments to writing theses. I still run these catch-ups every two months as we pursue PhD and industry jobs in different parts of the world.

Since July 2026, I have volunteered with Digital Seniors, an organisation that helps older people build confidence with technology and stay connected. I volunteer for two hours, two days a week, helping with everything from setting up email accounts and online visa applications, to organising digital legacies.
